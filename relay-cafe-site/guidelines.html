Community Guidelines

Last updated: March 1, 2026

Relay.cafe is built for simple, one-to-one human connection. These guidelines exist to keep it safe for everyone.

1. Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited:

  • Hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, or disability
  • Harassment or bullying
  • Threats or incitement of violence
  • Sexual exploitation of minors
  • Explicit sexual content
  • Promotion of illegal activity
  • Encouragement of self-harm or suicide

2. Enforcement

We have zero tolerance for content that violates these guidelines.

  • Reported messages are removed immediately.
  • All reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Accounts that violate these guidelines may receive strikes.
  • Accounts that accumulate multiple strikes may be temporarily suspended.
  • Severe violations may result in immediate suspension.
  • Repeated or extreme violations may result in permanent suspension.

Temporary suspensions may persist even if an account is deleted and recreated. Suspensions automatically expire after a defined period without further violations.

3. How to Report

You can report a message directly within the app using the "Report" button. Reports are anonymous and handled promptly.

4. Legal

We may cooperate with law enforcement where required by applicable law.

5. Contact

To report abuse or for safety concerns, contact us at safety@relay.cafe.
