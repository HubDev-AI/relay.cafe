# 🛡 What Your Terms Must Cover (Non-Negotiable)

For Relay.cafe specifically, you must address:

### 1️⃣ No Responsibility for Content

Users are responsible for what they write.

You are not liable for:

* Illegal statements
* Defamation
* Threats
* Confessions
* Harmful content

---

### 2️⃣ No Guarantee of Delivery

You must clearly state:

* Messages may not be delivered.
* Messages may expire unread.
* The service does not guarantee receipt.

This protects you if:

* The pool is empty.
* A decryption error occurs.
* Infrastructure fails.

---

### 3️⃣ Limitation of Liability

You must limit damages.

Example language:

* Service provided “as is.”
* No warranties.
* No liability for indirect or consequential damages.

---

### 4️⃣ User Conduct Rules

You need to prohibit:

* Illegal activity
* Threats of violence
* Harassment
* Spam
* Attempts to break the system
* Automated abuse

Even if you don’t actively moderate,
you must legally reserve the right to remove users.

---

### 5️⃣ Account Termination Rights

You must reserve the right to:

* Suspend accounts
* Block users
* Restrict access
* Without explanation if necessary

---

### 6️⃣ Intellectual Property

Clarify:

* Users retain rights to their messages.
* You only process them for relay purposes.
* You do not claim ownership.

---

### 7️⃣ Privacy Reference

Link to Privacy Policy.

Must explain:

* What you collect (Apple ID hash, session data).
* What you do not store (messages beyond 24h).
* Data deletion.
* Logging practices.

---

# ⚖️ Important Reality

Even with Terms:

You can still be subpoenaed.

You can still receive legal notices.

Terms reduce liability.
They do not make you immune.

---

# 🌍 Jurisdiction Matters

You need to specify:

* Governing law (which country/state).
* Dispute resolution venue.
* Whether arbitration applies.

This depends on where YOU are legally located.

Where are you operating from? Country only is fine.

That affects how we draft it.

# 🌿 1️⃣ You Must Add Links to Terms & Privacy

Inside the app, you need:

* A link to Terms of Use
* A link to Privacy Policy

Best place:

Settings screen (where “Delete account” already exists)

Add:

* Terms of Use
* Privacy Policy

These can open:

* In-app Safari view
* Or external browser

You don’t need to clutter the UI.
Just make them accessible.

This is important for:

* App Store review
* GDPR compliance
* Legal defensibility

---

# 🌿 2️⃣ Tiny Copy Adjustment (Optional but Smart)

On first launch, below the onboarding text, you can add a very small line:

> By continuing, you agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Small font.
Muted.
Non-intrusive.

This creates explicit agreement.

Not dramatic. Just formal.

---

# 🌿 3️⃣ Content Responsibility Boundary (No UI Change Needed)

You do NOT need to add:

* Warning banners
* Content disclaimers before reading
* “User-generated content may be disturbing”

That would change tone.

Your Terms already cover that legally.

Keep the app quiet.

---

# 🌿 4️⃣ Abuse Handling (Silent, Not Visible)

Internally, ensure:

* You can suspend a user.
* You can block a user.
* Rate limits exist.

You don’t need UI for that.
Just internal enforcement capability.

---

# 🌿 5️⃣ Error Handling Must Be Calm

Make sure:

If decryption fails or KMS fails,
the user sees:

> Unable to open message.
> Please try again.

Not:

* 500 error
* Technical detail
* “Encryption failure”

You already handled that tone well.

---

# 🌿 6️⃣ Data Deletion Integrity

When user deletes account:

* All user records gone.
* Sessions gone.
* Tokens gone.
* Messages already in pool remain anonymous.

This is already aligned.

No change needed.

---

# 🌿 7️⃣ Screenshot Policy

You already decided:

* System doesn’t prevent screenshots.
* System doesn’t encourage sharing.

No change needed.

---

# 🌿 8️⃣ Do You Need Age Restriction?

Technically yes.

You should include in Terms:

> You must be at least 16 years old (or applicable minimum age in your country).

Because:

* GDPR
* Anonymous messaging
* Potential sensitive content

Add that to Terms.
No UI change required.

Minimal checklist:

* Ensure error handling remains non-technical.
* Ensure account deletion is irreversible and clean.
